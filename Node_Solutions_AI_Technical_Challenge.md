**The mock problem**&nbsp;

A growing professional-services company receives client requests through email, website forms, and chat. Team members manually read each message, decide how urgent it is, determine who should handle it, and write an initial response. Important requests are sometimes delayed or sent to the wrong person.&nbsp;

**Your job is to create a working prototype that makes this process faster and more consistent.**&nbsp;

**What your solution must do**&nbsp;

\- Accept a written request as input.&nbsp;

\- Return a short summary of the request.&nbsp;

\- Assign one category: Sales, Support, Billing, Technical, or Other.&nbsp;

\- Assign a priority: Low, Medium, High, or Urgent, with a brief reason.&nbsp;

\- Route the request to one owner: Sales Team, Client Success, Finance, or Engineering. \- Draft a professional first response that a team member could review and send.&nbsp;

\- Present the results in a clear interface or workflow.&nbsp;

**Rules and expectations**

| Use any stack&nbsp; | Python, JavaScript, workflow tools, AI builders, or another approach are all acceptable. |
| ----- | :---- |
| **AI use is  allowed** | You may use AI while building. You must understand and be able to explain every important part of your solution. |
| **Keep it  practical** | A focused working prototype is better than a large unfinished system. Do not spend money to complete this challenge. |
| **Protect  information** | Use only the mock requests below. Do not use private, confidential, or real client information. |
